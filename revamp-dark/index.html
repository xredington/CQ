What is an ISV?

The companies powering the cloud economy

An ISV — Independent Software Vendor — is any company that develops, owns, and sells its own software. Most ISVs today build cloud-native SaaS products sold on subscription, from global names like Salesforce and Zoom to specialized regional companies serving specific industries.
